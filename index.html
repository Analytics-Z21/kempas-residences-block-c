Location

Where the city disappears around the bend.

Genting Highlands, at a different altitude and a different pace. Three reasons people buy here.

You already know the hill. What matters here is that the building is finished, the unit is furnished, and the keys are the only thing left to collect.

What you do not have to do

There are no construction timelines to wait out and nothing to fit out. Completion was Q4 2025 and every unit comes fully furnished, down to the blackout drapery.

Low-density by design, so the sky deck, the garden cafe and the hiking trail are shared with fewer neighbours. Daily conveniences sit within a kilometre. The resort is 13km away when you want it, and out of earshot when you do not.

Buying for your parents means buying certainty. Completed, freehold, furnished and secured, so the only variable left is which weekend they move in.

Built for people who are not there every day

Digital access with multi-mode entry and guest authorisation. A guard house at the gate. Hotel-style energy management in the unit, and an EV charging point on site for the drive up.

The Karak Expressway is 8.8km away. Close enough for a Sunday visit, far enough that the air is a different temperature when you arrive.
