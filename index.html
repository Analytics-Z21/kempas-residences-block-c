    USP

    What sets Kempas-C apart.

    A completed, low-density freehold condominium in the greenery of Genting Highlands, sold fully furnished.

    Completed and move-in ready

    No construction timelines. No uncertainty.

    Low-density, private community

    Fewer residences. Greater discretion. Designed for long-stay owners and those who value quiet continuity.

    Resort-inspired comforts

    Hospitality-inspired spaces, crafted for repeat stays and everyday living rather than short-lived novelty.

    Buy for

    Where the city disappears around the bend.

    Genting Highlands, at a different altitude and a different pace. Three reasons people buy here.

    You already know the hill. What matters here is that the building is finished, the unit is furnished, and the keys are the only thing left to collect.

    Completion was Q4 2025 and every unit comes fully furnished. Low-density by design, so the sky deck, garden cafe and hiking trail are shared with fewer neighbours. Daily conveniences are a short drive away, and the resort is 11.8km up the road when you want it.

    A morning round in the cool air, then home to a unit that is already set up. Awana's 18 holes are 11 minutes away, and your clubs can live here.

    Fully furnished, freehold and completed, so a golf weekend needs no more planning than the tee time. For an evening out, the Awana SkyWay up to the resort is 12 minutes away.

    Buying for your parents means buying certainty. Completed, freehold, furnished and secured, so the only variable left is which weekend they move in.

    Digital access with guest authorisation, a guard house at the gate, hotel-style energy management in the unit and an EV charging point on site. The Karak Expressway is 7.6km away: close enough for a Sunday visit, far enough that the air is cooler when you arrive.
