Location

Cool air, closer than you would think.

Genting Highlands sits at a different altitude and a different pace, a real climate shift from the Klang Valley rather than a themed getaway. Block C is part of the wider Kempas Residences masterplan, in the same low-density hillside setting that made the original development a quiet retreat rather than a resort strip.

Quiet, but not cut off

The nearest amenity is a 350m walk. Daily conveniences sit within a kilometre. The expressway is 8.8km away, and the resort itself is 13km when you actually want it, which is the point: near enough to reach, far enough to be quiet.
